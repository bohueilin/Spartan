SAMPLE DATA

What it actually does

One number in. One plan out.

Reads

Your recovery, from WHOOP

Connect WHOOP or import your own CSV export. Until you do, everything you see is sample data — and it says so on every surface that shows a number.

Decides

Four bands, one decision

Below 34 is a recovery day. 34–49 goes lighter. 50–66 is steady, quality work. 67 and up is your window to progress. The thresholds are in the source, not a black box.

Explains

Every action names its reason

Each item on the plan tells you why it's there and which signal it moves — and hands you a specific follow-along session when movement is the answer.

Protects

Says stop when you should stop

Report pain and the plan eases for the next few days, then lifts on its own. It will never sell you through an injury, and it never diagnoses anything.

On the phone

Calm enough to use every morning.

The part most apps skip

Honest by construction.

It stays on your phone

No accounts, no servers, no analytics SDKs. Export everything as plain text, or delete all of it — data, tokens and scheduled reminders — in one action.

Sample data is labelled

Fabricated numbers wear a SAMPLE DATA chip everywhere they appear, including in the screen-reader output and the exported file.

No streaks to break

Consistency is shown as seven calm dots. There is no chain to lose, no confetti, and no copy that makes a missed day feel like failure.
